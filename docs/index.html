PLAIN ENGLISH

How to read this study

You don't need to know blockchain analytics to follow the main idea.

What counts as a user?

We count NEAR accounts, not people. One person can control several accounts. An account is active when it signs at least one transaction that week.

What is a cohort?

A cohort is a group of accounts first seen in the same week. Think of everyone who joined a club during the same week.

What does “came back” mean?

The account signed at least one transaction in the selected week after it first appeared. Receiving tokens without signing does not count.

Why compare the first eight and last eight groups?

It gives an early-versus-late comparison using the same number of weekly groups. The confidence interval helps show how uncertain that difference is.

What is the confidence interval?

It is a range that shows how uncertain our estimate is. If the range includes zero change, we cannot confidently say retention went up or down.

Are high-transaction accounts bots?

We do not know. The filters only test whether very busy accounts change the result. They are not a bot detector.
