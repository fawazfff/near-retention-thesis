Read this carefully

On-chain return is a proxy.

A wallet that does not transact again may still be using NEAR apps in ways this dataset cannot observe. Multiple accounts may belong to one person. The analysis measures visible account behaviour, not unique human users.

Retention can support an investment thesis. It cannot prove product-market fit on its own.
